## Capabilities
27 routes cover search filters, real utilities, selected knowledge cards, comparison, an orbit lab, games, visual Easter eggs, travel, shopping and media search. Jev classifies natural language; local explicit syntax is labelled Search syntax. Selecting from `/` is immediate and labelled Selected by you, and the next query edit returns to automatic routing. No silent keyword fallback.

Real local tools: calculator/tip splitter, unit converter, timer, stopwatch, metronome, RGB color picker, orbit calculations, two-player tic-tac-toe and a jumping dinosaur runner. Sound requires explicit Start. Special knowledge UIs support one or two curated cases: weather in Singapore/Tokyo, serendipity/ephemeral, Interstellar/Dune Part Two, Earth/Mars. Weather uses free Open-Meteo; currency uses free ECB rates via Frankfurter. Both endpoints only accept whitelisted identifiers. Unsupported queries remain honest Google links; never show unrelated default facts. Flights and hotels have editable itinerary/stay controls; shopping has budget and product preferences. Finance includes a local compound-growth calculator and stock research controls. Video and news have actionable source/date refinements. Images show two attributed NASA references with image-search controls. Maps load a real OpenStreetMap city preview only on explicit request. Outbound Google searches preserve the visitor’s query and preferences. Never fabricate prices, availability, headlines or search results.

The landing page has no header brand or Classic/Fluid switch. Notes is in the footer with ShapeShift and author Anish Gupta credit. The landing page has no example grid or visible disclaimer paragraphs. Type `/` or tap the slash control for the searchable keyboard-accessible menu of 43 distinctly named examples. Notices, privacy, limitations and manual mode lock live in Notes. Source badges and source disclosures remain available with active tools.
